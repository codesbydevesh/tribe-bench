## How to read this

A NO-GO from a statistic only means "no effect larger than its floor". `spatial_z` is the retracted primary (G020, D027) — its floor is the number that makes the 2026-07-31 verdict uninterpretable. Any verdict reported without the corresponding floor beside it violates doctrine **D-3**.

Caveat, stated plainly: these floors are computed on a **synthetic** brain whose mass structure is modelled on the printed top-k ROIs, not on the real prediction cache. They are correct for the *design*; the constant relating them to real TRIBE units is unverified until S2 produces real predictions.
